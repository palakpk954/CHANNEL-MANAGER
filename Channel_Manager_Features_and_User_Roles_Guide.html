1. Executive Summary

The Hospitality Cloud Channel Manager provides synchronized real-time two-way distribution of hotel room inventory, rates, restrictions, and bookings across major Online Travel Agencies (Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Agoda, MakeMyTrip) and front-desk walk-in operations. The system features a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model across 6 distinct user roles to guarantee data integrity, staff accountability, and operational efficiency.

Architecture Rule 6: Zero Overbooking Guarantee
When a booking occurs on any channel (or front-desk walk-in), the system atomically locks the inventory unit and pushes availability decrements across all other connected channels in under 60 seconds, eliminating manual parity updates and double bookings.

4. Role-Specific Feature Breakdown

4.1 Organization Admin & Super Admin

  • Multi-Property Governance: Instant switching between properties via the header switcher.
  • New Hotel Onboarding Wizard: Interactive 4-step wizard at /onboarding for rapid property setup.
  • OTA Channel Connectivity: Connect Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Agoda, MakeMyTrip, and iCal calendar feeds.
  • Demo Data Seeding: 1-click toggle to seed realistic sample reservations and demand curves for staff training.
  • System Audit & SLA Compliance: Complete audit inspection to ensure compliance with the < 60s sync SLA.

4.2 Property Manager

  • Live Operations Dashboard: Real-time occupancy percentage, room breakdown, arrivals, and departures.
  • Overbooking Conflict Resolution (Rule 6): Instant alerts and guided room reassignment drawer when simultaneous OTA bookings occur on the final room.
  • Maintenance Room Holds: Block rooms for repairs or owner stays (Shortcut: H) which immediately decrements sellable inventory across all OTAs.
  • Room & Rate Mapping: Match internal room types to OTA listing codes.
  • Instant Sync Now: Force an immediate outbound push to all connected channels.

4.3 Revenue Manager

  • 90-Day Interactive Calendar Matrix: Visual calendar showing rooms, prices, sold units, and restrictions with color tokens.
  • Single-Cell Direct Quick Updates: Adjust nightly rates, toggle stop-sell, or enforce Minimum Length of Stay.
  • Bulk Rate Wizard: 4-step wizard (Shortcut: B) to adjust prices across custom date ranges and day-of-week filters with confirmation guardrails.
  • 1-Click Bulk Undo: Revert accidental bulk price mistakes instantly with full transaction history restoration.

4.4 Reservations Staff (Front Desk & Reception)

  • Today's Movements: Filter today's arriving and departing guests for smooth check-ins and key handovers.
  • Walk-In & Phone Bookings: Click "+ New Booking" to instantly record walk-in guests, syncing outward to OTAs in < 60 seconds.
  • Unified Bookings Inbox: Toggle between "Guestbook View" (visual cards) and "Compact Table View".
  • Reservation Cancellations: Process cancellations with recorded reasons, automatically restoring room availability everywhere.

4.5 Read-Only Auditor & Stakeholders

  • Occupancy & Revenue Oversight: Real-time visibility into occupancy rates, revenues, and demand curves.
  • Rate Parity Verification: Audit published rates across all dates without modification risk.
  • Sync Log Inspection: Audit payload summaries, HTTP status responses, and latency records.
